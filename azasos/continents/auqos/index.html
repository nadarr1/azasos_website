  • Arthec

    The butchers of Auqos, they butcher the animals that are imported from the Iriesaadi. They then prepare the meat and export it to other tribes and nations.

  • Crualxis

    The warriors of Crualxis, they import metal weapons from Lireon, and sell them trained warriors. The Crualxis have some of the best individually trained soldiers. Though they are less trained in group formations, so they must be trained accordingly.

  • Iriesaadi

    The masters of domesticating the creatures of the Auqosian wilds. They sell exotic animals to rich collectors and to various governments and zoos. They also sell some of these animals to the Arthec.

  • Kolxor

    The hunters of Auqos; they are extremely skilled at hunting big game, especially dangerous ones. They sell the goods they gather from the hunting to other tribes and nations.

  • Nyssaadi

    The archers and fletchers of Auqos. Their bows and arrows are expertly made and are a valuable commodity. Nyssaadi archers are also hired as mercenaries for both the Vraulikoth and Caeloran armies.

  • Taric

    The farmers of Auqos. They grow all sorts of exotic crops, herbs, and drugs for medicinal and recreational purposes. The herbs and medicinal drugs are a valuable commodity for many nations in the world, and are heavily exported to Lireon for their hospital network. The crops are traded are mostly exported to Lireon and Caelora.

  • Shaphis

    The Oracles of the people of Auqos. They excel at using divination magic. So much so that even people from other countinents seek out their help for various problems. This is their primary source of trade, that and their pottery.

  • Tree of Life

    A tree that the tabaxi of auqos see as holy and that it was bestowed with great wisdom from Aaris. It acts as neutral ground for the tribes and as Auqos's capital. Many come here to pray for their needs and praise Aaris.

Brief History

Auqos is populated by a species of humanoids known as tabaxi. They are feline-human hybrids, (and the species is unique to Azasos). Aaris gave them a massive tree that they worship as holy; they think that it is the only way to gain Aaris's favor. The tribes eventually split apart into their seperate regions of Auqos, and started to specialize.

Language

The tabaxi speak the language of Uvo, which is very simplisitc in nature. Hand gestures are very important to gain meaning through the words spoken. Because of the use of complex hand gestures paired with a very vowel-heavy vocabulary that has to be pronounced almost perfectly, it is the hardest language to learn on Azasos.
