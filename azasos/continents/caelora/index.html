Brief History

Caelora used to house the capital city of Azasos until Aaris left Azasos and left his daughter in charge of the world. After this They became an independent nation, with the Mystic Knight Order sworn to protect their king. They remained united after the death of Eana, and only went to war when it was absolutely necessary. Then as the world was starting to trade again, the High King rose to power and conquered the Mystic Knight Order. He then proceeded to kill the king and seize the throne of Caelora. Since then there have been whispers of a resistance rising against the dictator, who was now seated on the throne.

Language

Caeloran is the main language of Caelora. Its unique use of noun and verb modifiers as the languages adjectives and adverbs sets it apart from the rest of the languages of Azasos. All adjectives and adverbs are attached to either the front or the back of the world they are modifying. If there are multiple adjectives or adverbs modifying a word, some scribes put the first one out front as a separate word while others just attach both to the front. Caeloran is heavily inspired by the language Aaris first spoke to the people, and remains the closest to it.

The Mystic Knight Order

Mystic Knights were the protectors of Azasos bestowed with blessings from Aaris. They followed his ways and based the Virtues they recite on his words and actions. They rely on training with both practical combat skills and magic to make them capable warriors and protectors of the peoples of Azasos. They were prized fighters in the Battle for Azasos that eventually led to the banishment of Ahameous, the God of Evil.
