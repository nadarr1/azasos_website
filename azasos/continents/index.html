The Continents of Azasos

Hover over the name of each continent to view a map of the continent and a brief paragraph on it.

  • Lireon

    The central continent of Azasos, and the center of trade.

  • Caelora

    The western continent of Azasos, which is home to the elves.

  • Khare

    The eastern continent of Azasos, the second human nation.

  • Auqos

    The south-western continent of Azasos; a place of lively jungles and dangerous creatures.

  • Thelwihr

    The northern continent of Azasos, home of the mountain dwarves and the greatest forges in the world.

  • Vraulikoth

    The southern continent; home to the best shipbuilders in Azasos.

Text contained within "( )" is information that is unknown to the people of Azasos.
