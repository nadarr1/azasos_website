  • Akhenaton

    Population: 701,000
    Description: Khare's connection to Thelwihr. All sorts of metal and metal products are imported through the port city that is located near the Shield. The city exports a wide range of Khare's products to Thelwihr, and even to Lireon.

  • Ihalo

    Population: 2,507,000
    Description: The capital city of khare and the most populated city in all of Azasos. The city is built near the western river that makes up the western side of the Alypius River Valley. This area has very fertile farmland due to the location and the irrigation that has been installed. Ihalo farms a large variety of crops including: sugarcane, grains, corn, etc. In recent years the upper parts of the Alypius River Valley have been found to be rich with near surface gold. This sparked a gold rush which flows down into Ihalo to be traded and shipped to other continents.

  • Kema

    Population: 689,000
    Description: A city located in the upper regions of the Alypius river valley, and is a starting place for people looking to get in on the gold rush. The city also is the starting place for the most popular touring destination in Khare, due to its proximity to the Eye of Khare. Just like Ihalo, the city farms quite a bit, although not as much as Ihalo.

  • Zranta

    Population: 324,000
    Description: A city that primarily focuses on farming because of its location near a river deltat. The space near the river is rich with nutrients that makes for strong and healthy crops. All the excess crops are sent south to either Akhenaton or Kema.

Brief History

A large group of Lirei people wanted to break free from Aaris's direct control. They wanted freedom, so they moved East to an uninhabited continent. They eventually established their own language and culture. After Aaris's absence and Eana's death, Khare became the primary enemy of Lireon because they thought that the Lirei killed the Queen. The people of Khare respected Eana more than Aaris, and were willing to follow her. This relationship eventually improved through trade and diplomacy.

Language

Kharean is the national language of Khare, and is derived from Lirei. The same language convention is used, but the name pieces that are used are slightly different than the Lirei equivalents.
