  • Aledence

    Population: 415,000
    Description: A major port city on the eastern coast of Lireon. It connects the east to Lireon, and its major tradeway, Aaris Way, which eventually moves goods across Lireon and to the West.

  • Eahcester

    Population: 453,000
    Description: A city on the western coast of Lireon. The majority of the city is constructed between the mainland and an island near the coast. The city's foundation is made out of cobblestone that is around 100 feet thick and is held up by massive pillars that go to the bay's bottom. There is a canal that runs through the middle of the city, which allows for trade ships to unload their goods and for transportation through the city. The city was constructed on the water so that they could use what little fertile land they had near them to plant crops. Eahcester is the major trade port that connects the west to Lireon.

  • Eleygate

    Population: 1,224,000
    Description: The capital of all of Azasos until the death of Eana, and after that it became the capital of Lireon. The Eley River flows through Eleygate, which is what gives Eleygate its name. The city is built in multiple ringed layers all separated by massive walls; all built outward from the castle which is at the center of the city. Each wall is guarded by hundreds of soldiers that patrol the wall all day.

  • Esrine

    Population: 897,000
    Description: Being built near a mountain, the walls of Esrine go from cliffwall to cliffwall, and come out into a semicircle that forms the borders of “downtown” Esrine. This semicirlce of wall is on the side of the mountain that faces inward, towards Lireon and away from the sea. Esrine is the forging capital of Lireon; a large percentage of processed metals and metal products are forged in Esrine. In addition, a large amount of metals are mined in the mines around Esrine.

  • Prathe

    Description: The major farming city in Lireon. The farms around it produce the majority of local grown grain and vegetables. The city is built on a spring, which is where the city gets its water. A complex irrigation system brings this water to the majority of the farmland near the city.

Brief History

After the death of the Queen of Azasos in 300 AC, the continents turned on each other, some acusing others of assassinating the queen. This led to wars, and the eventual fracturing of Lireon. The continent was now split into thirteen smaller kingdoms. Over four hundred years later, the descendent of the previous king of Lireon, Eveddre Star, united the country after a war campaign. He was praised for his battle tactics. He also put into place a council elected by the people; they in turn elect the king. He also rebuilds international relations, which starts a new golden age of commerce.

Language

The main language of Lireon is Lirei. Lirei names are formed by a base, a noun, and any number of prefixes or suffixes which modify the noun. For example the name "Sendre", is divided into the base: "sen", which means gift. And the suffix: "dre", which means glorious. And together "Sendre" means glorious gift. Lirei names don't have to have a prefix or suffix, and can have any number of either, but most parents try to keep it simple when naming their children. The bases, prefixes and suffixes are simplified versions of real words in the Lirei language.
