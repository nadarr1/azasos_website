History

The dwarves of Thelwihr conquered the Halgrin Dorn Mountains, the biggest mountain range on Azasos. They excel in mining the mountains and refining the mined metal into weapons, tools and other ultility items. They export both the unrefined metals and the metal goods to other countries. Farming is almost impossible in Thelwihr, so they have to import all their crops from other nations. They do have steady reserves of cattle and chickens however.

Language

The dwarven language of thelwihrian is paired with a runic written language that is completely different to the left-to-right written systems used by the central nations. It is written from top to bottom with each rune representing a noun, verb, or describing word.
