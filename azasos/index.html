Welcome to Azasos

What is Azasos?

Azasos is a fictional world, in the universe of Azura. I started to design the world in october of 2021, and have continued to do so on and off for the last year and a half. Throughout that time the world has gone through a ton of game-changing developments, which allowed me to further flesh out the world, and make it more believable. The world is still in the early stages of development, meaning that all content on this page is subject to change in the future. I hope you enjoy the worldbuilding displayed on this website, but know that this is just the tip of the iceberg for all of what Azasos truly could offer.

Brief History of its Creation

I first decided to create Azasos when I was playing Dungeons and Dragons with my friends. I saw the amazing world that my Dungeon Master had created and was inspired to do the same. So in October of 2021 I set off on a journey to create the world that my mind imagined. At first, I created the world for use in a Dungeons and Dragons campaign that I wanted to run. But after complications with my players schedules, we could not play. Then in August of 2022 I had an amazing idea: write a novel. It started with an idea for one character that eventually exploded into an amazing plot. After writing the first draft of the novel, I started to outline the second while fleshing out the world more and more as I outlined. And that brings us to present day, and the current use of Azasos!
