The Myst

A force of power that can be drawn from the body and some powerful artifacts. It is the energy that allows Myst-casters to use their magic. (It is the magic power in which the universe was created and is primarily stored in a location called the Heart of the Multiverse.)

Myst-casters

People who can wield the power of the Myst and use it for various purposes. Among these people is another group called the Navizar, who have a greater potential in the magic that they can use. (Myst-casters have a few changes in the biology of their nervous systems which allow them to draw the Myst from the Heart and wield it in the physical world.)

The Luset Academy

The Luset Academy is a global organization that prides itself on teaching the ten schools of magic to anyone who can both pay the tuition and has the potential to wield the magic. It may be expensive to get an education through the academy, but it breaks down the ten schools into manageable pieces that can help focus a student's mind.

The Luset Academy was established after the death of Eana, the former queen of Azasos in an attempt to unify the teaching of magic against the now-splintering Azasos. Many magic schools across Azasos, all joined together and went under one name.

The Swords of Power

To help defend the world, Aaris gave the world about three hundred swords of power, which were divided among the continents. In addition, he gave one more to each leader of the six continents that are more powerful than the ordinary swords of power. And finally, he gave one to each of the twelve members of the Circle: the leadership council of the Mystic Knights

Each sword is made of an unknown metal (that contains trace amounts of Genesis stone). In addition to this, the metal is extremely hard, and flexible and can keep its edge for a long time. Magic users can draw from the Myst (Magic Energy) that is stored in the blade, just like how they use the Myst in their bodies.

Text contained within "( )" is information that is unknown to the people of Azasos.
