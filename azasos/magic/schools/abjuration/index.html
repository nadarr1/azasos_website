This school of magic focuses on using Magic Energy to protect the user or people and objects around the user. Diamonds can be used to focus the Energy so that not as much Energy is wasted upon using the School. The Luset Academy and many other smaller magic schools teach three ways to use Abjuration Magic or three different barriers that protect from various things.

Invisible Barriers:
Abjurers can use Magic Energy to create invisible barriers that can block physical attacks, as well as Energy created from Evocation Magic.
Mental Barriers:
Abjurers can also create "mental barriers" which protect the user from the effects of Enchantment Magic. Using "mental barriers" requires more experience with Abjuration Magic to effectively use.
Temporal Barriers:
The final and most complex way an abjurer can use their Magic Energy is by creating "temporal barriers" that prevent people from using Divination Magic to see into the user's future, past or present. This ability requires near mastery with Abjuration Magic.

Text contained within "( )" is information that is unknown to the people of Azasos.
