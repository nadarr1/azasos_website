Amelioration is the second school of magic and it focuses on using Magic Energy to heal biological material. Seana gemstones can be used to focus the Energy. This school is extremely useful to society and supercharges hospitals located around Azasos. The Luset Academy divides Amelioration into several pieces. No one in the present year has the ability to heal a major brain injury because of the complexity of what needs to be repaired.

Primary Healing:
This is the most basic form of Amelioration. It focuses on healing damaged tissue.
Greater Healing:
A step up from primary healing is greater healing, which is more difficult to master and generally requires more Magic Energy to use. Ameliorators can reform bones and repair damaged organs with greater healing. However, it is impossible to reverse aging (except if an absurd amount of Energy is used in combination with Creation magic: Cultivation).
Bio Healing:
This piece of Amelioration Magic can be used to get rid of intoxicants, viruses, and bacteria in someone's body. This form of Amelioration is the hardest to master because of the intricacies involved in performing it. An inexperienced user might accidentally do more harm than good to the body they're trying to heal.

Text contained within "( )" is information that is unknown to the people of Azasos.
