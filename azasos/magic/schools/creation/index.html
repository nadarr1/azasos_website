The most powerful and Energy hungry school of magic. By using creation magic, the user can create matter from Magic Energy. This type of magic is what allows "gods" to create the celestial bodies in the multiverse, but can be almost impossible for a magic user on Azasos---even a Navizar ---to create something bigger than a small bead. People can create bigger objects, but they would have to use their Energy for weeks, months, or even years, depending on what they want to create. Training in creation magic can allow the user to make more complex objects with their Energy. Creators can use Ameorl gemstones to focus their Magic Energy.
