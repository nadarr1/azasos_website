Development is the fifth school of magic, and it focuses on changing the appearance and properties of objects by changing their chemical and or physical structures slightly. The Luset Academy and other magic schools generally divide this school of magic into two forms: appearance changes and property changes. Jade gemstones can be used by developers to focus their Magic Energy.

Appearance Changes:
The easier form of Development to use. It allows developers to change the outer appearance of an object. For example, development could be used on clothing like dye to change the color of the clothing. Doing something like this doesn't cost much Energy because of the relative simplicity of the effect. Like property changes, this form uses bond changes to create their effects, but less thoroughly than property changes. It can also only be used to add color, not take it away.
Property Changes:
A more complex form of Development that requires larger quantities of Magic Energy and more extensive training. This form can be used to form chemical bonds in a substance. An example of this is taking carbon in a substance such as coal and bonding it to oxygen atoms in the air.
