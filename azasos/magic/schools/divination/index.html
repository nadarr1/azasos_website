Divination is a more mystical and less understood school of magic. It deals with the revealing of information in multiple ways. One of the ways Divination reveal information is by allowing the user to understand both verbal and written language. The other way is by looking into the future. Diviners can use opal gemstones to focus their Magic Energy.

Understanding Language:
The easiest way to use divination magic. Diviners can understand verbal and written language. It can be tricky to understand language when a diviner first starts, but becomes easier after they practice. They can also use this ability to help solve riddles and break codes hidden in writing or verbal communication.
Seeing the Future:
This form is hard to use and can be very unpredictable at times, especially when magic is used to disrupt the events a diviner sees in the future. In addition to these limitations, powerful abjurers can also protect themselves with the use of temporal barriers. Despite these drawbacks, divination is still extremely powerful. For example it can be used to see a few milliseconds into the future during a fight, so the diviner can position their body and weapons to make themself nearly unbeatable on the battlefield. However, it gets harder to see into the future, the farther a diviner wants to see, and requires more Energy to do so.
Seeing the Past:
Like seeing the future, this form can be relatively hard to use, but is generally a little more predictable than seeing the future, because the events portrayed have already occurred. This can be useful for diviners because it allows them to literally learn from the past because they can see events as they happen (as long as the area isn't protected by a temporal barrier). It gets harder to see into the past, the farther the diviner is both physically and temporally from the target location and time.
Seeing the Present:
Like both of the two previous forms, it can be relatively hard to use. This form is more predictable than the other two because the events are happening at the time the user is using this form. This can be useful to spy on people or places and is mostly used in surveillance or to uncover secrets. It gets harder to see the present the further the user is physically from the target location.
