Enchantment is a very complex school of magic that deals with the intricacies of human emotion and its various quirks. An enchanter can use Magic Energy to both amplify existing emotions and create entirely new emotions in the subject. Onyx gemstones can be used by enchanters to focus their Magic Energy.

Amplifying Emotion:
An enchanter can manipulate the emotions in a target's mind with their Magic Energy. They can make someone angrier or calmer for example. The only drawback to this side of Enchanting is that the target has to already be feeling these emotions at least a little.
Creating Emotion:
an enchanter can create emotions in a target's mind with more training and Magic Energy. This can be used to inspire people without hope for example. It is much harder to create emotions that weren't there, or were almost nonexistent.
