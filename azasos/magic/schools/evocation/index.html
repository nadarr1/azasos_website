Evocation is a school of magic that focuses on turning Magic Energy into various other forms of energy (electric, heat, radiant...) except motion energy. An evoker can use volrase gemstones to focus their Magic Energy. The school is generally divided into a few subsets by the Luset Academy and other magic schools.

Energy Magic:
evokers can use Magic Energy to create Energy such as heat to warm things for example. This is a very simple way to use the Magic and requires very little practice. However, there is a more complicated way to use it by combining it with the use of creation magic. Using creation magic, a Myst-caster can create fuel and heat the fuel with Evocation magic to create fire. This is one of the many ways Evocation magic can be paired with Creation magic.
Sending:
Evokers can use Magic Energy to send telepathic messages to other sapient creatures. This is a very difficult ability that costs more Energy the further the evoker tries to send the message. Evokers can send messages only based on physical descriptions and names, but that is more difficult compared to sending a message to people the evoker is familiar with.
