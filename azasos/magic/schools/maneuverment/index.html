Maneuverment is an important school of magic for combatants and involves moving objects in a few ways. Sapphire gemstones can be used to focus a maneuverer's Magic Energy.

Increased Speed:
A maneuverer can use their Magic Energy to boost the kinetic energy in movements around them. It is primarily used to boost one's own speed, but can also be used to increase the speed of someone or something else's movements. This can be used to increase an object's falling speed for example.
Teleportation:
the second way a maneuverer can manipulate Maneuverment is by teleporting. Using Magic Energy in this way is harder, and requires more Energy. A maneuverer can teleport any person or object to any desired location. The number of people or objects, and the distance to the location both contribute to how much Energy is required to teleport.
