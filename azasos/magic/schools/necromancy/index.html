The last, and arguably most straightforward but difficult school of magic is Necromancy. Its main ability is to reconnect someone's soul to a body. The Luset Academy teaches one way that Necromancy can be used. Necromancers can use phoenix gemstones to focus their Magic Energy.

Bonding Souls:
when someone dies their soul leaves their body (its connection to the Heart slowly dwindles; once the connection is completely gone, it goes to the Heart). A necromancy can bring back a soul to its body (using Magic to strengthen its bond). However, if a body is not healed, the soul will try to leave again. In order to properly revive someone, their body has to be healed and the soul has to be brought back.

(A necromancer can align a soul on a new body. They can also unbind souls from metals and rebind them to a body.)

Text contained within "( )" is information that is unknown to the people of Azasos.
